INVITATION FOR BIDS
Procurement of IT Equipment for the Regional Training Institute, Islamabad
Tender No. RTI/PROC/2026-14                          Issued: 05 October 2026

Sealed bids are invited from firms registered with the Federal Board of Revenue for the
supply of desktop computers, printers and networking equipment.

Bids must be submitted through e-PADS on or before 2026-10-28 at 11:00 hours. Bids will be
opened the same day at 11:30 hours in the presence of bidders' representatives.

SECTION II - ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

2.1 The bidder shall possess a valid National Tax Number (NTN) issued by the Federal Board
of Revenue and shall appear on the Active Taxpayer List at the time of bid submission.

2.2 The bidder shall be registered for sales tax and shall provide a valid Sales Tax
Registration Number (STRN).

2.3 The bidder shall be a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2017 and shall have
been in continuous operation for at least five (05) years.

2.4 The bidder shall have a minimum average annual turnover of Rs. 5 million over the last
three financial years, supported by audited financial statements.

2.5 The bidder shall have completed at least three (03) similar supply contracts with
government departments during the last three years, each of a value not less than
Rs. 2 million.

2.6 The bidder shall not be blacklisted by any federal or provincial government department,
and shall submit an affidavit to this effect on stamp paper of Rs. 100.

SECTION III - BID SECURITY AND SUBMISSION

3.1 Each bid shall be accompanied by a bid security of Rs. 150,000 in the form of a pay
order or bank guarantee issued by a scheduled bank in favour of the Director (Finance).

3.2 The bidder shall maintain an account with a scheduled bank in Pakistan and shall submit
bank statements for the preceding twelve months.

3.3 Bids shall remain valid for a period of ninety (90) days from the date of bid opening.

3.4 The following documents shall be attached with the technical proposal:
    (a) Copy of NTN certificate
    (b) Copy of Sales Tax Registration certificate
    (c) Certificate of incorporation issued by SECP
    (d) Audited financial statements for the last three years
    (e) Copies of purchase orders or completion certificates for similar contracts
    (f) Affidavit regarding non-blacklisting
    (g) Copy of CNIC of the owner or authorised representative

SECTION IV - TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

4.1 The bidder shall provide a manufacturer authorisation letter from the original equipment
manufacturer for all quoted items.

4.2 The bidder shall hold ISO 9001 certification, or provide evidence of an equivalent
quality management system.

4.3 The bidder shall provide an after-sales service facility located within Islamabad or
Rawalpindi, with a response time not exceeding forty-eight (48) hours.

4.4 Equipment shall carry a minimum warranty of three (03) years, on site.

4.5 Bidders may, at their option, offer extended warranty terms; such offers shall not
affect technical evaluation.

SECTION V - EVALUATION

5.1 Bids will be evaluated on a least-cost basis among technically responsive bids, in
accordance with the Public Procurement Rules, 2004.

5.2 A bid that does not meet any mandatory eligibility requirement of Section II shall be
declared non-responsive and shall not be evaluated further.

5.3 The procuring agency may reject all bids at any time prior to the acceptance of a bid,
in accordance with Rule 33 of the Public Procurement Rules, 2004.
